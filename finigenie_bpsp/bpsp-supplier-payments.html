Settlement architecture

Keep the money movement where it belongs.

The source architecture positions Finigenie as the technology and orchestration layer, while the acquiring partner handles regulated acquiring and settlement. Supplier funds are routed directly to supplier bank accounts rather than through a merchant collection-and-disbursement loop.
